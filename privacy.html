Privacy policy for rclone-personal

This setup accesses the owner's Google Drive file contents and metadata to list, download, upload, update, and delete files as directed by the owner's synchronization settings.

Synchronized copies are stored on the owner's computer and in their Google Drive. OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's local rclone configuration. This setup does not sell data or use it for advertising.

The owner can revoke access through their Google Account settings. Revoking access does not delete previously synchronized files; those copies must be managed separately.
